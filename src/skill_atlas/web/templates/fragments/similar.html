How similarity scores work

Scores compare names (20%) and descriptions (80%) using shared words and phrases. Higher percentages indicate closer metadata matches. Bars use red below 40%, amber from 40–69%, and green from 70–100%. Values are rounded to whole percentages. They are not probabilities or quality ratings.
